5

Before trusting any result we checked the simulator against published numbers. The two-rotor and three-rotor-loss equilibria from Mueller and D'Andrea's ICRA paper match every digit they published. The three-rotor case is a strong check because it uses all the gyroscopic terms. For the one-rotor case, thrust, spin rate and tilt match within half a percent, but the pitch rate is six percent lower; we found that the paper's own published values leave a residual in its own equation 12, so that one published case is slightly inconsistent. We also wrote an independent implementation of their more detailed IJRR model and reproduced all five failure cases, and matched a second drone, the Bebop 2, from Sun et al. The one real gap: in flight, the vehicle spun up 30 percent faster than the model predicts. That matters for the delay results later, and we will study its sensitivity.
