We designed two LQR controllers: a normal hover controller, and a failure-mode controller at the spinning equilibrium using the paper's weights, with motor states and altitude added. On the full nonlinear model with motor lag and thrust limits, the failure controller recovers from a perturbed start: tilt and altitude settle in one to two seconds, the spin rate in about four. One number to note: the failure controller tolerates only about 18 milliseconds of pure input delay, compared with 40 for the healthy one. The slowest oscillation, at the spin frequency, is lightly damped.
