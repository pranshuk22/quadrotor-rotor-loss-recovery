Here is the main prediction result. On held-out episodes from the training plant, the residual model has the lowest error at every horizon from 50 milliseconds upward. At one second its error is 0.42, against 0.70 for full-state EDMDc and 5.1 for the analytical model, which is worse than simply assuming nothing changes, because the unmodelled thrust loss makes it drift. Our proposal claimed that full-state EDMDc is unstable; on the failed vehicle we confirm that it has eigenvalues outside the unit circle and that it eventually loses to the no-change baseline, after two to three seconds. Note also that at three seconds even the residual model loses to no-change: long open-loop horizons need feedback, which is why the MPC re-plans every 5 milliseconds.
