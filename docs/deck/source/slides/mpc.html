Now in closed loop. We built a linear MPC on the analytical model, and the same MPC with the residual added as a measured disturbance, evaluated outside the optimization so the QP stays convex. On the training plant, the thrust loss causes a steady altitude error of about 29 centimetres for LQR and analytical MPC. The residual-Koopman MPC cuts it to 19 centimetres, a 32 percent reduction, close to the 35 percent Todde et al. report for healthy flight. Each QP takes about 5 milliseconds. On the held-out plant all three are similar. When we used the MPCs to catch the vehicle after a detection delay, they did not beat LQR. The problem there is that the linear model is not valid far from the spin, not the constraints. The obvious next baseline is integral action, which we will add.
