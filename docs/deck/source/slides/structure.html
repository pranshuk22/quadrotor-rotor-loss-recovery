Linearizing about the spinning equilibrium in body coordinates gives a linear time-invariant model. The open loop has two undamped oscillations, at 10 and 19.6 radians per second, the slow yaw mode, and the altitude double integrator. It is fully controllable from the three rotors, which reproduces the paper's result. On observability: gyros alone cannot see tilt or altitude. A small finding of ours: because the spinning equilibrium is tilted, vertical acceleration depends on tilt to first order, so gyro plus barometer is enough in principle. But the observability Gramian shows it is about 2,700 times weaker than measuring tilt directly, so it is a structural property, not a practical estimator.
