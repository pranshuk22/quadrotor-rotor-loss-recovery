When one rotor dies, a quadrotor cannot hover level any more. Mueller and D'Andrea showed it can still fly by spinning steadily about a tilted axis, called the relaxed hover. But there is a gap in time: the fault detector needs some time tau to notice the failure. During that time the normal controller keeps flying a vehicle that has lost a rotor. Only after detection does the failure controller take over and try to capture the vehicle into the spinning state. All guarantee-based papers we found assume tau equals zero. The one published tolerance figure is empirical and for a different vehicle.
