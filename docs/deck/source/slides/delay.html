This is our key result so far for RQ2. We simulate the Mueller and D'Andrea strategy: spin up on two rotors, then switch to the LQR. With instant detection it loses only 0.4 metres of altitude, which agrees with their flight. But with delay, things fall apart quickly: 17 of 20 random starts are safe at 10 milliseconds, 6 at 20, 1 at 30, and none at 50. Real detectors take 30 to 130 milliseconds. Two honest caveats: with a looser criterion, attitude recovered only, the tolerance is about 40 milliseconds; and our model spins up more slowly than the real vehicle did, so we present these numbers as valid for the published model and will study the sensitivity.
