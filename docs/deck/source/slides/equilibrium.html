This is the equilibrium the failed vehicle must reach. With rotor 4 gone, the two side rotors produce equal thrust and the opposite rotor about half of it. The vehicle then spins at about 19 radians per second about an axis tilted 16 degrees from the body z axis, and the centre of mass moves on a circle of only about 7 millimetres. The figure shows how the equilibrium changes with the thrust ratio; it reproduces the paper's Figure 3.
