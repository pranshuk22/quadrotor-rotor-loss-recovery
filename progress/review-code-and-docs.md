# Critical review: code, experiments, tests and documents (2026-10-10)

Reviewer stance: adversarial. Every finding below is backed by a command output, an equation or a quote. "Suspected" marks anything not fully reproduced. Re-runs were made in a separate copy of the repository; nothing under `results/`, `docs/` or the source tree was modified.

## Summary

- **Counts:** 1 critical, 7 major, 15 minor, 12 nit.
- **Code is mostly sound.** Physics, sign conventions, quaternion and n = Rᵀe₃ kinematics, the Jacobians, the ZOH/filter consistency, the delay-line matrix and the Hu–Lin LMIs are all correct. All 45 tests pass. V0, E1–E4 and E6 regenerate byte-identical tables; E7 matches except the wall-clock QP times. 64 random states inside the certified E5 ellipsoid all recover in the nonlinear simulation.
- **The headline RQ2 number is fragile (critical).** "The published strategy tolerates ≈ 10 ms" comes from a spin-up thrust of mg/2 = 2.45 N per rotor, hard-coded in `SpinUpThenLQR`. The paper's own flight (Fig. 4) uses ≈ 2.95 N. With 2.95 N, the empirical τ\* doubles to 20 ms and the τ = 0 altitude loss drops from 0.41 m to 0.03 m.
- **The "model spins up 30% slower than flight" gap is a modelling artefact, not aerodynamics.** It has the same cause: with the flight's spin-up thrust, the model needs 0.64 s against 0.62 s in flight. Separately, the "0.4 m loss, consistent with the paper's flight" claim compares two different quantities.
- **Several document claims contradict the tables:** "residual best at every horizon ≥ 50 ms", "randomized training makes them worse (on B)", "the thrust loss causes the 0.29 m bias", and "10 ms tolerance" while the Monte Carlo is 17/20 at 10 ms. These claims appear in the report, the slides, the docs and the proposal.

## Verified correct (checked, no problem found)

- Rotor layout, propeller spin signs and reaction torques τ_i = (−1)^{i+1} κ_τ f_i match ICRA 2014 eqs. (8)–(9) and (23) (ω̄₁ < 0).
- `body_rate_derivative` equals ICRA eqs. (12)–(14) term by term, including the gyroscopic sign −I_zz^P q ω_Σ / +I_zz^P p ω_Σ and the use of I^T in the cross term and I^B on the left (checked against the PDF text).
- Closed-form relaxed-hover rates (p = 0, r = κ_τ(f₁ − f₂ + f₃)/γ, q = l f₂/((I_zz^T − I_xx^T) r + I_zz^P ω_Σ)) re-derived by hand.
- The paper's own one-rotor numbers leave −0.0113 N m in eq. (12) (recomputed); the inconsistency claim is correct.
