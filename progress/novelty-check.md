## 3. Suggested rewording

**3.1 Gap statement (report.tex §1 ¶"RQ2 was added…", proposal §1 [added] and §2, novelty-and-claims §3).**
Current: "every recovery method with a guarantee that we found assumes that the failure is detected at the instant it happens. The only published tolerance figure we found is empirical and for a different vehicle."
Suggested: "Most guarantee-bearing recovery methods assume instantaneous detection [Simha 2017; Al Lawati 2026]. Başak and Prempain [ECC 2015; Başak 2018] account for a fixed detection delay in a Lyapunov/LMI stability analysis of a linearized quadcopter that loses a motor. Their analysis ignores thrust saturation and does not compute the largest tolerable delay. Passive schemes avoid detection altogether [Ke 2023; Chen 2025]. Reported tolerances are empirical: 180 ms for a variable-pitch vehicle [Kulkarni], and active FTC recovering with 0.1–0.18 s detection delay in simulation and flight [Chen 2025; Zhou 2026]. We did not find a *certified maximum* delay under saturation for the relaxed-hover regime."

**3.2 C1 wording (novelty-and-claims §4).**
Current: "To our knowledge, the first certified (rather than simulated) bound on the tolerable fault-detection delay."
Suggested: "To our knowledge, the first certified *maximum* tolerable detection delay τ\* for complete rotor loss that accounts for thrust saturation and the drift of the undetected phase. It extends stability-under-detection-delay analyses for linear switched models [Başak 2015/2018]." Lower the "est. chance unpublished" from ~75–80% to about 50–60% until Stephan 2018 and Qi 2016 are read.

**3.3 C2 wording.**
Current: "A capture set for the relaxed-hover controller valid from hover onset."
Suggested: "A saturation-aware invariant ellipsoid for the relaxed-hover controller, made robust over the spin-up yaw rate by vertex LMIs. LPV synthesis on the yaw rate [Stephan 2018] and SOS recoverable sets at the relaxed hover with rotor-speed limits [Qi 2016] exist. Our contribution is to use such a set as the capture target for a detection-delay budget." Report.tex §RQ2 ("This is the motivation for the next step…") should cite Qi 2016 and Stephan 2018 at that point.

**3.4 Detector-delay citation (report.tex abstract, §1, §2, §RQ2; overview; proposal §5, §6).**
"Reported detectors need 30–130 ms [ieee2025mml1]" should cite Strack van Schijndel, Sun & de Visser (arXiv:2102.06439), which reports the 30–130 ms range. If MM-L1 is kept, cite it for "≈ 45 ms" [Yang et al., RA-L 2026]. Fix both bib entries (`ieee2025mml1` authors; `lee2024koopmanftmpc` → Lee, Kim, Kim, Bang, ICCAS 2024).

**3.5 The 10 ms result (report.tex abstract and conclusions, overview, proposal §5).**
Current: "the published recovery strategy tolerates only about 10 ms of detection delay … while reported detectors need 30–130 ms."
Suggested: "In our simulation of the published model, the published spin-up-then-LQR strategy meets our pre-registered safety criterion (≤ 2 m altitude loss, no inversion) up to about 10 ms of detection delay, or about 40 ms if only attitude capture is required. Detectors reported for propeller loss need about 30–130 ms [Strack van Schijndel 2021]. Nonlinear controllers have recovered with 0.1–0.18 s of detection delay [Chen 2025; Zhou 2026], so the limit is specific to this strategy and model." Also keep the caveat that the model spins up 30% slower than the real vehicle.

**3.6 Claim (b), report.tex §RQ2 "for any tuning of the healthy controller".**
Suggested: "for any healthy controller whose allocator keeps running motors at or above idle thrust (as standard autopilots do), and near hover where gyroscopic terms are negligible." The bound fails if the healthy controller is allowed to stop rotor 2 (the simulator stops a motor for u ≤ 0).

**3.7 RQ1 wording (report.tex Related Work last ¶, proposal §2).**
Add DEKC (arXiv:2506.08319) to the list of physics + Koopman-residual work for quadrotors. Add Sun/Zhang/Lang 2022 as learned-failure-model MPC for complete rotor loss. Keep "We did not find residual Koopman models applied to the complete-loss, spinning-equilibrium regime". That sentence survives this check, with the caveat about Wauters et al. (ECC 2026, unread). Change "nor a certified bound on the tolerable detection delay" to "nor a certified *maximum* tolerable detection delay under thrust saturation (Başak 2018 certifies stability for a given delay in a linear model)."

**3.8 Link claim (proposal §6 "This answers the future work Todde et al. state").**
Suggested: "This applies LMI certificates with bounded learned error, as in SafEDMD [Strässer et al.], to a residual-Koopman controller of the failed vehicle." "Answers" overstates what is a direct application of known machinery.

**3.9 Observability (report.tex §Linear).** Add one sentence: "Practical estimators of the spinning vehicle use the accelerometer with rotation correction [Sun 2021; Solanki & de Visser 2022]; our gyro + barometer result is a structural remark only." No novelty should be claimed.
